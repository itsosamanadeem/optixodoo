Purchase Control Hub

Purchase Control Hub extends Odoo purchasing with stronger governance across purchase requests, approvals, budgets, analytics, serial tracking, and master data.

Odoo 19 Purchase Requests Budget Control Serial Import Analytic Distribution

Why Use This Module

This module is designed for companies that want tighter procurement control without splitting the process across many disconnected customizations. It improves how teams raise purchase requests, route approvals, validate departmental budgets, generate RFQs, and track incoming serialized items.

Key Features

Department-Based Purchase Requests

Collect product lines by department, assign department managers as approvers, and support partial refusal handling.

Purchase Order Approval Chain

Work with the linked purchase approval flow and add manager review actions, sent-back handling, and activity notifications.

Budget Enforcement

Apply budget rules by department with restrict, warning, or allow behavior before final purchase approval.

Automatic Analytic Distribution

Fill analytic distribution from department analytic account, city, and GL settings on purchase request and order lines.

Smart RFQ and PO Creation

Create purchase orders from approved request lines using vendor, product, and department grouping logic.

XLSX Serial Import

Import serial numbers directly into stock operations and support asset tag handling during receipts.

Master Data Controls

Generate vendor and product codes automatically, add CNIC on partners, and track product warranty and asset tag fields.

Role-Based Visibility

Separate SCM user, SCM manager, CEO, and purchase permissions to keep the procurement process structured.

Process Flow

Purchase Control Hub supports the full path from purchase request submission to manager review, RFQ creation, budget validation, and serialized receiving.
